### UnimeType

**One keyboard for all your languages — no switching keyboards across apps or mid-sentence.**

Keep a clean ASCII layout for single-key shortcuts in Figma or Blender, `\cite{}` commands in Overleaf, WASD controls in games, WhatsApp / Excel quotes alongside WeChat or LINE chats, and terminal/IDE workflows—then press **Caps Lock / Convert** to convert romanized text in place.

- **On-Device Transliteration 2.0 Toolkit & `short-v3` Protocol**: **[github.com/UnimeType/transliteration](https://github.com/UnimeType/transliteration)**
